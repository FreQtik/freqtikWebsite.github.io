Impulse Anvil

DESIGN THE IR ITSELF

Impulse Anvil lets you take IRs, recordings and found sounds, reshape them, combine them, and save the result as a new WAV impulse response.

Load → Shape → Combine → Edit → Bake → Reuse

Where do you want to start?

Make something in 5 minutes Load a sound, change it, and Bake your first IR. Try creative recipes Phase tricks, found sounds, recursive Bakes and multi-morph stereo worlds. Combine A and B Choose how two sources become one new response. Draw movement & route time Draw your own A→B transition or use Path to move forward and backward through source-time.

The basic idea

A normal convolution workflow often starts with:

Which IR do I want to use?

Impulse Anvil adds another step:

What IR do I want to make?

A room IR can be your starting point. So can a guitar cabinet, a metal hit, a voice, a scrape, a field recording, or another IR you already created in Anvil.

Once you hear something worth keeping, press Bake. The result becomes a WAV IR you can use again.

How these docs are written

The main pages use producer language: what a control does, what changes in the sound, and what to try.

Deeper DSP details stay inside Technical background sections so the normal workflow remains quick to read.
