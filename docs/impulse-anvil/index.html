Impulse Anvil

THE IR IS THE THING YOU ARE MAKING

Impulse Anvil is an impulse-response design workstation. Load IRs, recordings or found sounds as material, decide how A and B should relate, then Bake the response you make into a reusable WAV.

1. Your sound goes in.A vocal, drum, synth, guitar, field recording or anything else on the track.
2. An changes it.The response can add filtering, resonance, space, reflections or stranger transformations.
3. Anvil changes the IR.A and B are material. Morph defines their relationship. Draw, Path, Glue and Omni can author how that relationship develops.
4. Bake keeps the result.Your experiment becomes a reusable WAV impulse response.

The useful mental shortcut: when you reshape the IR, you reshape the transformation that will be applied to your sound.

Material → Relationship → Movement → Sculpt → Bake

Where do you want to start?

Make something in 5 minutes Get from an empty plugin to your first useful Bake without learning the whole manual. Learn Anvil by doing Work through short optional quests, unlock the next task, and keep a local history of what you have learned. Choose the A/B relationship Start with the human idea behind each Morph mode, then open Technical details only when you want them. Try creative recipes Use focused examples when you want a destination instead of another control description.

How these docs teach

The learning pages use one information order:

Why→Do→Notice→Understand→Technical details

You should be allowed to understand the idea before you are expected to know the specialist vocabulary. Reference pages stay compact for returning users.

Technical words with a dotted underline can be hovered, focused with the keyboard or clicked to reveal a short explanation. These definitions are optional; they are not prerequisites for continuing.
