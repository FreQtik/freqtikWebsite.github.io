Interaction

  • Click A, B or Morph to choose the audition state.
  • Use Focus View when one work area needs more room.
  • Shift + drag = fine movement; Ctrl + drag = very fine movement.
  • Global Undo/Redo tracks deliberate workstation edits; the curve U remains local to authored routes.

Bake scope

Bake includes the prepared IR state, including Morph/Lerp/Draw/Path/Glue/Omni, Edit, EQ, Width, IR In, Normalize and IR-side limiting.

Input, Wet Level, Dry/Wet and final Out are playback controls and are not embedded in the exported IR.
