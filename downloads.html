Installation

Extract, install, create.

VST3: Close your DAW. Extract the full ZIP and copy the complete Impulse Anvil.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\. Rescan in your DAW.

Standalone: Extract the whole ZIP, keeping its Resources folder with the executable. Launch the application; no host or license is required.

Upgrading: Back up the old VST3 and important DAW projects. Keep externally loaded IRs at their saved paths and test an existing project copy before replacing a working setup.
