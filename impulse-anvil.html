Impulse-response design · Freeware · v1.0.125

Design the response.
Hear the difference.

Impulse Anvil is a free impulse-response design effect. Load impulse responses or compatible recordings, reshape and morph them, and hear the result on your track through its built-in convolution engine. Export your designs as reusable WAV impulse responses.

Download Windows VST3Download Standalone

Windows 10/11 x64 · 127 included factory IRs · Full features · No purchase, activation or account required

What it does

One response, many possibilities.

Use one IR on its own, or prepare A and B to design relationships between two responses. You can listen while you edit—no separate convolution plugin is needed.

Shape an IR

Trim, stretch, reverse and refine response material with Edit, EQ, Color and output controls.

Morph A and B

Explore 21 Morph relationships, including time, spectral and residual approaches.

Author movement

Use Draw, Path, Glue and Omni to define how two responses interact over time.

Listen and export

Audition with internal stereo convolution, save a preset, or Bake a reusable 32-bit float WAV IR.

How to begin

Make your first response.

01 — Insert. Put the VST3 on a playing track or open the Standalone.

02 — Load. Choose one of the 127 included factory IRs, or load compatible material into A. Add B when you want to morph.

03 — Explore. Shape the response while listening. Save the setup or Bake the IR when you want the file itself.

Free to use

No license system. Just sound design.

Impulse Anvil v1.0.125 runs without activation or license checks. Existing Cubase projects using the older commercial VST3 can generally load with the same plug-in identity; back up projects and the previous plug-in before upgrading.
