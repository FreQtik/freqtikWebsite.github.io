Impulse Anvil · Freeware · v1.0.125

Create impulse responses
that go beyond reverb.

A free IR editing and morphing workstation for sound design, Foley, post-production and electronic music.

Transform recordings and existing impulse responses into new resonances, textures and spaces. Shape one response, combine two, or draw how they transform across a single IR. Hear the result through built-in convolution, then export it as a reusable WAV.

What can you make?

Start with a sound. Make it something else.

Turn recordings into sound design

A metal impact, an unusual room or an environmental recording can become the starting point for new resonances, textures and spaces. Trim, stretch, reverse, EQ and shape the response while you listen.

Make two responses become something new

Load A and B, then explore how their character combines. Blend familiar spaces, build unexpected hybrids, or discover a response neither source produces on its own.

A curve that becomes sound

Draw movement inside the IR itself.

Imagine a sharp metallic attack gradually giving way to a spacious tail. Draw how the response moves between A and B over its own duration, then Bake that evolving result into one WAV impulse response.

The curve shapes the exported response—not an automation track that has to keep running during playback.
