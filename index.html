Featured audio software

Impulse Anvil.

An impulse-response design workstation for Windows VST3, built to turn IRs, recordings and found sounds into reusable convolution material.

Build from two sources

Start with IRs, recordings or found sounds and prepare A and B before they meet.

Author the relationship

Choose how A and B relate, then write movement with Morph, Draw, Path, Glue or Omni.

Bake and reuse

Refine the useful result, Bake it as a portable WAV IR, then reuse it or build another generation from it.
